Airtel 5G NSA Analysis & LTE Band Masking Technical Report

Research case study · by Shubhomoy · October 2026

Overview

This report looks at how Airtel's non-standalone (NSA) 5G network behaves in practice, focusing on the LTE 1800 MHz (Band 3) anchor channel and the radio metrics reported by NetMonster.

Background

In NSA mode, 5G NR runs alongside an LTE network. The LTE cell carries control signaling (the anchor), while the 5G cell adds extra data capacity. This is why a phone can show a 5G indicator while its signaling still depends on an LTE band.
